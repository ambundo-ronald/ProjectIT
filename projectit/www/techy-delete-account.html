Delete your Techy account

Your Techy account belongs to the company that gave it to you ({{ company }} for this site). You can ask for it and your personal data to be deleted here, or in the app (Account, then Delete my account).

What happens

  • The company checks the request is yours, switches your account off, and deletes your personal details (name, email, phone, photos of you, your locations) within 30 days.
  • Records the company must keep by law or for its business (for example invoices, attendance and the work done on a customer's machine) are kept, without your contact details where possible.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your phone.

See also the privacy policy.
