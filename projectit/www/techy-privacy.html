Techy App

Privacy policy

Techy App for Android and the web · Last updated {{ updated }}

Techy App is the field-service app companies use for their technicians, managers, contractors and customers. You sign in to your company's own Techy site (for example {{ company }}), with an account your company gives you. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices.

Who is responsible for your data

The company whose site you sign in to decides what is recorded and keeps the data on its own site. It is the controller of your data. The app sends what you enter only to that site and to nowhere else.

What the app collects and why

The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not sell or share data with third parties, and it does not use your data for anything except the work above.

Permissions on your phone

  • Location: asked the first time you check in. If you refuse, you cannot check in from the app, but everything else works.
  • Camera: asked the first time you take a photo.

Security

Everything is sent over an encrypted connection (https). Your password is not stored on the phone. The phone keeps a sign-in key, which you or your company can cancel at any time. Work saved for use without signal stays in the app's own storage and is sent when you are back online.

How long data is kept

Your company keeps work records as long as it needs them for its business and the law (for example invoices and attendance). Signing out removes your sign-in key from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can see and correct your details through your company. To have your account and personal data deleted, use Delete my account in the app, or the account deletion page. Your company then deletes your account and personal data within 30 days, except records it must keep by law.

Children

Techy App is a work app for adults and is not meant for children.
